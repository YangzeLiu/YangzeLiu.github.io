About

I am a full-time research assistant at Shandong University, advised by Prof. Zhongyi Han. I received my B.S. in Computer Science + Philosophy from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2025. I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

I study LLM-based agents, using controlled experiments to understand what actually drives their behavior and performance: where the knowledge in an agent's memory comes from, whether a model is coupled to its harness, whether a benchmark rewards the decisions it claims to test, and what goes wrong in an agent's actions even when it passes its task. I want to use this understanding to build better agents that can run safely in real environments. Earlier I worked on data selection for efficient training.
